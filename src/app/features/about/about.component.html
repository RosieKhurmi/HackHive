Challenges

Participant Guidelines

Flexibility

We are encouraging participants to use any technologies or services they are comfortable with, adjusting as necessary to align with their solution's needs.

Collaboration

Work in teams of up to 4 members to brainstorm, design, and build your solution.

Support

Mentors and resources will be available throughout the event to guide participants.

Submission Guidelines

Option 1: Architecture Diagram Challenge

Design a detailed architecture diagram for a solution to your chosen challenge. Focus on:

  • Key Challenges: Identify and address critical issues within the topic.
  • Services and Tools: Choose technologies that align with your solution's goals.
  • System Flow: Clearly illustrate how data moves within the system.
  • Scalability and Security: Ensure the design is secure, reliable, and can grow as needed.

What to Submit - Presentation Deck Requirements:

  1. Title Slide:
    • Project Name and Team Members
  2. Problem Statement:
    • Clearly define the problem within your chosen category and its significance
  3. Comprehensive Diagram:
    • Showcase a detailed architecture diagram with clear annotations explaining components, data flow, and system interactions
  4. Design Rationale:
    • Provide a written explanation of your design choices, how they address the identified challenges, and why specific tools, frameworks, or services were selected
  5. Scalability, Security, and Reliability:
    • Highlight how your architecture ensures scalability, maintains security, and ensures reliability for the proposed solution
  6. Innovation:
    • Emphasize any innovative features or approaches used in your design
  7. Video:
    • Create a video (not exceeding 2 minutes) to walk through your presentation and visually explain your architecture

Option 2: Platform Development Challenge

Develop a functional platform that addresses challenges in your chosen topic. Focus on:

  • Functionality: Build a solution that works effectively for your target audience.
  • User Experience: Prioritize a clean, intuitive design for users.
  • Impact: Ensure the platform contributes positively to solving the problem.

What to Submit - Presentation Deck Requirements:

  1. GitHub Repository: Submit the link to your GitHub repository, ensuring it includes:
    • Codebase: Well-organized and documented code implementing your platform.
  2. README File:
    • Instructions for setting up and running your platform.
    • A description of the platform's purpose and key features.
  3. System Overview:
    • Highlight key components of the platform (e.g., backend, frontend, AI models, APIs).
  4. Scalability and Security:
    • Discuss how the platform ensures scalability and security, including any frameworks or best practices followed.
  5. Presentation (Optional):
    • Title Slide: Project Name and Team Members.
    • Inspiration: What inspired your project and how it relates to the chosen category?
    • Coding Journey: Briefly discuss your development process, challenges faced, and how they were overcome.
    • Learnings: Highlight key takeaways from your experience.
  6. Demo Video:
    • Include a video (not exceeding 5 minutes) showcasing a working model of your platform, demonstrating its features and functionality.
