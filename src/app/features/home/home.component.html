What's HackHive?

A thrilling 3-day journey where creativity meets cutting-edge technology!

This year's theme, “AI for a Better Tomorrow,” challenges participants to harness the power of artificial intelligence to solve real-world problems and enhance everyday life.
Whether you're a seasoned coder or an aspiring designer, this hackathon offers an inclusive platform for innovation and collaboration.

At HackHive 2025, participants will tackle some of the most pressing challenges in today's world by designing AI-powered solutions.

The event is structured to allow students to explore their chosen topic through one of two pathways:

  • Architecture Diagram

    Design a robust, scalable, and efficient architecture to address challenges in your chosen topic. Focus on data flow, security, scalability, and reliability while illustrating how various components interact.


  • Platform Development

    Build a functional platform—whether a web application, mobile app, or another digital solution—that addresses the challenges in your chosen topic. Focus on creating an intuitive, user-friendly interface and a solution that can drive meaningful impact.
